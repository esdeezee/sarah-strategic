You'll recognize it

You already hired the designer agency product manager — and it's still stuck.

Not because your team isn't smart. Not because the vision is wrong. Because nobody can see the whole system clearly enough to know where to push. It usually shows up as one of these:

The redesign that's going nowhere

Everyone agrees the website/product needs to change. Three kickoffs later, nothing has shipped and nobody can say why.

The initiative that keeps stalling

Real budget, clear vision, smart people — and a launch date that keeps moving for reasons no one can name.

Smart team, no movement

Every function is working hard. Nothing connects. Decisions loop back through the founder's head.

The hire that didn't fix it

You brought in the PM, the agency, the designer. Good people, real work — and the stuck thing is still stuck, because the function was never the problem.

The clock is external

An investor, an acquirer, a board, a client of your own — and staying stuck is about to get expensive.

Here's why this keeps happening: anything important now runs across functions, but org charts assign owners to silos — never to the connective tissue between them. So every function optimizes its piece while the thing that lives between them quietly dies. Hiring another function-shaped person can't fix a system-shaped problem. Someone has to read the whole thing.
