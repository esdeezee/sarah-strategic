Read & Align

What happens: I embed with your team for 30–45 days. I talk to everyone — leadership, the people doing the work, the people caught in between. I map the whole system. The diagnosis itself usually lands in the first two weeks; the rest is getting everyone to agree on it and making the fix stick.

What you walk away with:

  • The diagnosis. Where the system is actually broken, in plain language, with the evidence. Usually it's not where you think — that's exactly why you're stuck.
  • The roadmap. What to do about it, in what order, and who owns what.
  • The alignment infrastructure. The operating cadence, the shared source of truth, the decision paths — installed and running, not recommended in a deck. This is the moment your team exhales.
Fixed fee — $25,000 Scoped up for enterprise system size on the intro call. No hourly meter, no scope creep, no proposal theater. You know the price before we start, and you own everything I build.

What happens after: Sometimes Read & Align is all you need — the roadmap runs and I'm done. If there's more to build, we scope it then, from the diagnosis, with both of us knowing exactly what we're buying.
