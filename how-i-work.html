How I Work

Over 20 years and 100 client engagements, I've cultivated a process that works across large and small organizations. The marketplace, tech and tools may change. But this is evergreen.

Phase 1

Get Clarity

15–30 Days · Fixed Fee

What happens: I embed with your team, analyze data and documentation, and talk to everyone—leadership, the people doing the work, the people caught in between. Then I map out the whole ecosystem in order to make the diagnosis. Usually it lands in the first week or two; the rest of the time is getting everyone to agree on it.

What you get:

  • The Diagnosis: What the ecosystem actually looks like and where it's really broken in plain language and with evidence. Usually, it's not what you think; that's why you're stuck.
  • The Roadmap: A clearly focused strategic initiative that will have a cascading effect on solving all the ecosystem problems.
  • Next steps: Sometimes clarity is all you need, and the roadmap will run without me. But if there's more to build, then this is how we'll scope out Phase 2.
Fixed fee — $25,000 (Enterprise Scope)

The fixed fee is based on the complexity of the ecosystem and size of the business, which I'll clarify on the intro call.

All engagements start here—regardless of length.

Phase 2

Build Velocity

2–3 Quarters · Scoped from Clarity

What happens: You know exactly what you're building and buying. I stay embedded with your team. I lead and manage the strategic initiative's execution—get the systems built, the product launched, the website redesigned, the teams cross-functional, the strategy stable, the leadership advised, etc.

What you get:

  • The Thing Launched: Whatever the thing is that needs to be built to get you unstuck, it happens in scope, on time, and with a cascading effect across the ecosystem.
  • Human-centered Transformation: When people are empowered, know what to do, and how their contribution influences others, then they build on that, which transforms a siloed culture into a more resilient one.
  • Tooling & Systems Adoption: The key to any new tool—AI or otherwise—isn't the tool itself, but how people creatively integrate it into their processes and systems.
  • Strategy Connected to Execution: The plan and the doing stop being two separate things handed between two separate groups—which is how the companies that actually ship well operate.
  • Access to Sarah's Expert Network: I've worked at the leading-edge of technology for 20 years, so I've got the trusted network of talent to tap into if we need help.
Phase 3

Keep Momentum

1 Quarter · Advisory Retainer

What happens: The transformations get stress-tested against reality. I catch what breaks, adapt what needs to keep evolving, and set up the next strategic initiative for the roadmap to keep you scaling. Then I leave, on purpose.

What you get:

  • The New Diagnosis, Roadmap & Next steps: The new strategic initiatives that will keep you scaling into the next stage of success for your business, product, team and customers.
  • Scaling Human-centered Infrastructure: The team keeps a line to me so they can keep learning, growing, and getting more agentic across the ecosystem. At the same time, leadership and I make sure the strategy and execution stay bridged.
  • I leave by design: What I do runs without me; that's the goal! (But you can always call me back.)
