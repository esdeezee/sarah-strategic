How I work

Diagnose. Build. Hold. Leave.

Longer engagements aren't a separate service — they're the phases after the diagnosis, scoped from it, and they can only be bought through it.

Phase 1

Read & Align

30–45 days · fixed fee

The diagnostic. The only door in.

Phase 2

Build

2–3 quarters · scoped from the diagnosis

I stay in and execute: the systems get built, the teams get cross-functional, the thing ships. I'm not managing — I'm building the runway so you can fly without me.

Phase 3

Hold

One quarter

The system gets stress-tested against reality. I catch what breaks, adapt what needs to evolve, and set up the next roadmap. Then I leave. By design — that was always the goal.

Phase 4

On Call

Advisory retainer

The team keeps a line to me — to keep getting better, or to pull me back in when the next big thing needs the cycle again.
