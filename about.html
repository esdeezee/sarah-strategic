About

Sarah Strategic Consulting helps ambitious startups, global brands, and complex organizations successfully navigate the space between innovation and transformation. We partner with leaders who want to make life better—across the entire ecosystem—for everyone with technology.

A decade of strategic innovation and transformation

Founded in 2015, we began as a product & UX studio, building innovative and award-winning digital solutions outside of the Silicon Valley bubble with dedicated agency partners like Gensler, Deloitte, Five and Done, and Technology Humans & Taste. In fact, that independence still continues to give us an edge in helping clients discern hype from what actually matters.

In 2021, we organically expanded beyond product and UX into business transformation. Now, instead of just launching an app or website, we're the strategic operating partner that businesses call on to launch high-stake initiatives and build more sustainably—aligning people, priorities and processes. It's the same great work based on the same principles—just with greater impact and more positive outcomes for everyone.

Our Principles

Strategy Is Story

A strategy no one can retell doesn't build momentum — it's just a document. The best ones ripple past the room they were first presented in: into the systems, the process, the ecosystem, the launch, the redesign, the teams, and more. They keep running even after I'm gone.

Design Human Experiences

There's a human at the beginning of every business decision and a human at the end of every workflow and customer journey. Every tool, process, and product in between is a bridge, not a replacement for either one. I design for the people still standing there afterward.

Truth Over Hype

The fast answer feels like progress, but it just delays the decision a human still has to make. I'd rather tell you what's actually true and actually happening than what's fashionable or what you're hoping to hear. That includes about myself — I'll tell you what I don't know before I'll pretend otherwise.

Break the Template

What's the point of building the expected thing? My efforts will take you past the benchmark on purpose. I customize my solutions to your ecosystem because the obvious fix and the narrow fix were never going to solve what's actually keeping you stuck.

About Sarah Dzida the Person

Sarah Dzida is a creative writer, product builder, and strategy expert who's worked at the leading edge of technology for the last two decades. Her work spans across all kinds of industries, organizations, and domains. It's still being used and enjoyed by millions of people—even today. Currently, she's the engine behind this consultancy and the startup Hey Virgil.

In addition to her client work, she leads workshops and mentors professionals across all skill levels as near as Silicon Beach in Venice, Calif to as far as Budapest. She teaches graduate students at USC at the Annenberg School of Communication.
