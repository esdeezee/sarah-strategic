About

Since 2015, Sarah Strategic Consulting is the trusted partner of companies navigating product launches and operational chaos.

A decade of strategic innovation and transformation

Founded in 2015, I began as a product & UX studio, building innovative and award-winning digital websites, platforms and apps outside of the Silicon Valley bubble. My dedicated agency partners included Gensler, Deloitte, Five and Done, and Technology Humans & Taste. We also won some awards!

In 2021, executives started asking me to help connect strategic decisions to outcomes across team operations. That's how I organically expanded beyond shaping product, experiences, and brands to aligning people, priorities, systems and processes. It's the same great work doing the same great things based on the same principles—just with greater impact and more positive outcomes for everyone.

My Principles

Strategy Is Story

A roadmap is a strategy. And a strategy no one can retell doesn't build momentum — it's just a document. The best ones ripple past the room they were first presented in: into the systems, the process, the ecosystem, the launch, the redesign, the teams, and more. They keep running even after I'm gone.

Design Experiences for Agentic Humans

There's a human at the beginning of every business decision and a human at the end of every workflow and customer journey. Every tool, process, and product in between is a bridge, not a replacement for either one. I design roadmaps and engines that give people more agency to act rather than less.

Truth Over Hype

The fast answer feels like progress, but it just delays the decision a human still has to make. I'd rather tell you what's actually true and actually happening than what's fashionable or what you're hoping to hear. That includes about myself — I'll tell you what I don't know before I'll pretend otherwise.

Break the Template

What's the point of building the expected thing? My efforts will take you past the benchmark on purpose. I customize my solutions to your ecosystem because the obvious fix and the narrow fix were never going to solve what's actually keeping you stuck.

About Sarah Dzida the Person

Sarah Dzida is an independent product & ops specialist, digital experience expert, and creative writer who's worked at the leading edge of technology for the last two decades. Her work spans across all kinds of industries, organizations, and domains. It's still being used and enjoyed by millions of people—even today. Currently, she's the engine behind this consultancy and the publication Hey Virgil.

In addition to her client work, she leads workshops and mentors professionals across all skill levels as near as Silicon Beach in Venice, Calif. to as far as Budapest. She teaches graduate students at USC at the Annenberg School of Communication.
